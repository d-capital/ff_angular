Couple of details

Capital:

We need to know how much money you're willing to put into the portfolio so that we can allocate it between stocks, bonds and ETFs according to their prices and minimum lot sizes.

Exchange:

You can choose the stock exchange from whose stocks the portfolio will be assembled.

Asset Group:

Choose the assets to be analyzed: include safe bonds. Or buy only stocks of high-performing companies. Don't like the way oil prices move the markets? Exclude energy companies.

Prediction Method:

By default we will use the timeless classic - averaging, (ARIMA-GARCH) may soon be available as a paid feature.

Optimization method:

By default, we will use profit maximization with a risk constraint less than or equal to 2% (the so-called Markowitz optimization), The next option is to maximize the Sharpe ratio.

Optimization period


The recommended way is to choose last year to calculate the portfolio for next year. But you can choose your own range, for example, to simulate the portfolio's success in 2019 you need to optimize it on 2018 data
